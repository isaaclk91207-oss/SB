🌍 The Problem

After an earthquake, responders need to quickly prioritize which buildings to inspect first. Manual triage is slow, dangerous, and requires skilled personnel. In remote areas of Myanmar, this delay can cost lives.

💡 Our Solution

Safe AI provides instant visual triage from exterior building photos. Upload a photo → Get damage priority classification (Low/Medium/High) with Grad-CAM visual evidence. Works fully offline on a local laptop — no internet required during inference.

📋 How It Works

Select Language — Choose English or မြန်မာ (Myanmar)
Upload Photo — Take or upload an exterior building photo (JPG/PNG)
AI Analysis — ResNet18 classifies damage as Low / Medium / High
View Evidence — Grad-CAM heatmap shows what AI sees
Download Report — Get bilingual PDF with photo, result, and guidance

🏷️ Damage Classes

Class Meaning Action
Low No obvious visible exterior damage Professional review recommended
Medium Possible or minor visible damage Restrict access, arrange engineer review
High Severe visible damage Do not enter, urgent engineer review

⚠️ Known Limitations

• Small training set (150 images) — model may overfit
• Only trained on Myanmar earthquake photos
• Low light, indoor, or obstructed views may give unreliable results
• This is a triage aid, NOT a structural safety certification
